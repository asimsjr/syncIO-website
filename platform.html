Five modules

Sales

From lead sheet to signed deal, every step lives in one place and carries forward. Deals move through your pipeline stages; when one closes, the estimate and the project already know about it.

WHAT YOU SEE
  • Pipeline by stage, value, and owner
  • Lead sheets that become deals without re-entry
  • Where deals stall, and why

Estimating

The estimate is the budget, and the platform treats it that way — line items, price sheets, and a quote engine on the front end; variance tracking against actual cost once the job is live.

WHAT YOU SEE
  • Estimate-to-actual variance per job
  • Benchmark rates vs. what jobs really cost
  • Quote history per customer

Projects

Work in progress is measured, not guessed. Change orders, milestones, and time entries roll up to a live picture of each job — tied back to the estimate it started from and the invoices it's producing.

WHAT YOU SEE
  • WIP and unbilled work
  • Change-order impact on margin
  • Job health before it's a problem

Accounting

Invoices, payments, bills, and collections sync from your accounting system. The platform doesn't replace your books — it reads them and adds what they can't show on their own.

WHAT YOU SEE
  • AR aging and collection priority
  • Customer concentration risk
  • 13-week and longer cash-flow forecast

People

Labor is usually the biggest cost and the least understood. The platform costs every hour at the pay rate in effect when it was worked — raises included — so job margins reflect reality, not a blended average.

WHAT YOU SEE
  • Labor cost per job, period-accurate
  • Utilization and capacity
  • Overhead rate and margin floor
Intelligence Layer

It looks across your business, not just inside one system.

Most tools are experts in their lane. Your accounting system knows invoices; your project tool knows tasks. Neither can tell you that one customer's slow payments plus one job's labor overrun add up to a cash squeeze in week seven. The Intelligence Layer can, because it has all of it.

Cash-flow forecast

Week by week, booked versus expected, with the squeeze visible before it lands — not after.

Margin analysis

Per job and across the book. Trend, not just a point in time, so you see drift while there's still time to price differently.

Anomaly detection

Payment timing, vendor behavior, and job-cost patterns that don't match your own history — surfaced, not buried.

Nexus

Ask it like you'd ask your operations manager.

Nexus is the assistant built into the platform. Ask "which jobs are over budget?" or "what's our biggest cash risk this month?" and it answers from your own data — the same computations behind every screen — and shows its work. It's read-only by design: it can tell you, it can't change anything.
