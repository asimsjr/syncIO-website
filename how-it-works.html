How It Works

Connect. Sync. See. Act.

The Operational Intelligence Platform sits alongside what you already run. It doesn't ask you to replace anything, and your team keeps working where they work today.

1

Connect

Link the tools your business already uses. The platform reads from them; nobody changes how they enter data.

2

Sync

Data flows one direction, into your dedicated environment, stored as it came in. Nothing is re-keyed. Nothing is "adjusted."

3

See

The five modules and the Intelligence Layer assemble the operating picture: jobs, cash, margins, people, pipeline — together.

4

Act

Nexus and the module views surface what needs attention. Collections priority, job risk, cash squeeze, margin drift.

Onboarding

What the first few weeks look like.

Onboarding is a project, not a signup form. We provision your environment, connect your tools, load your history, and then work through it with you until the picture matches how your business actually runs. Expect a few weeks to get the data in, and a few more of you adjusting, correcting, and settling in before it feels like yours.

Discovery call

What you run on, what's not working, and whether the platform fits. If it doesn't, we say so on the call.

Your environment

A dedicated instance provisioned for your business — your own database, nobody else's data in it.

Connections

We link the tools you already use. Run on something we don't connect to yet? We build the connector as part of onboarding — the platform was built for that — and it stays yours.

First sync and walkthrough

Your history loads. We walk the screens with you and flag where your source data has gaps or inconsistencies the platform surfaced.

Adjustment period

You work in it. Names, categories, rates, and workflows get corrected to match reality. This is where it stops being a demo and becomes your operation.

Training and beyond

Hands-on with your team, by role. After go-live: support, connector updates, and new intelligence as the platform grows.

How it's built

Three rules the platform never breaks.

One source of truth

Every number has exactly one owner in the system. The same value cannot exist in two places with two different answers.

The screen never calculates

The display shows; the platform computes. That is how reconciliation errors go away — there is nothing to reconcile.

Data flows one way

From your tools, into your environment, into the picture. Never quietly back out into your books.
